I'm a PhD student jointly supervised by Yvain Nicolet at the IBS Grenoble as well as Sandrine Ollagnier at the LCBM in Grenoble, France. My research focusses on the evolution and mechanism of radical SAM enzymes. 

For my masters degree, I performed research in the Kamerlin Lab at the Georgia Institute of Technology in Atlanta, Georgia. There, my research focussed on characterizing the conformational dynamics of archaeal protein tyrosine phosphatases. 

My bachelors degree at the University of Bremen, Germany, first exposed me to the magic of structural biology and metalloproteins when I worked as an undergraduate researcher in the lab of Tristan Wagner. There I learned to use X-ray crystallography to gain structural insights into the mechanism and function of corrinoid proteins.
